Tom Clancys Rainbow Six Siege is one of Ubisofts games in the Tom Clancys Rainbow Six franchise. It was mainly developed by Ubisoft Montreal and was published by Ubisoft Entertainment.

The game is heavily inspired by real counter terrorist situations across the world. The multiplayer tactical shooter requires intensive team cooperation unlike other Tom Clancy's Rainbow Six single player campaign games. However there are still sinlge player situations availible offline.

The game was originaly released December 1st 2015 however an upgraded version of the game (Siege X) was revealed in february 2025 that would overhall all the games graphics, audio and mechanics.
